We facilitate connections and structured communication. We are not presenting the platform as a substitute for licensed real estate, conveyancing or legal services.

01 REQUIRED FOR EVERY LISTING

SEE THE WHOLE PROPERTY

A walkthrough that
shows what matters.

Every property listing must include a clear, well-lit walkthrough video. The person recording must speak as they move through the property and identify each room — not simply upload a silent montage.

  • Bright, steady footage with clear visibility
  • Each room named aloud as it is entered
  • Full interior coverage, including kitchens and bathrooms
  • Exterior, building entrance and relevant outdoor areas
  • Video must reflect the property's current condition

STRUCTURED COMMUNICATION

Keep the conversation
on the property.

Enquiries move into a monitored chat with a third-party agent overseeing the discussion. The chat is designed to keep conversations focused on negotiation — not exchanging phone numbers, email addresses or arranging private viewings outside the platform.

Initially, a human monitor will oversee conversations. An automated assistant may support this process in a later phase, subject to testing and escalation to a person.

WHEN YOU NEED MORE

Two routes forward.
Your choice.

A

Offer accepted

Receive a reminder that an accepted offer is not a substitute for the correct legal and transaction documents. You can request a partner agent to assist with documentation or arrange a physical viewing, with the relevant service and call-out fees disclosed first.

B

Offer not accepted

Ask for an agent to step in and help negotiate, or search for a similar property through the partner agency. If you choose the agency-assisted route, the applicable agency commission — including a 5% fee where relevant — will be explained before you proceed.

All services are optional. Percentage rental fees are calculated on the annual rent value; the full sale service is 2% of the agreed sale price. For rental services, the 2% of annual rent is the full-service fee. For a sale, the 2% may be shared by agreement between the seller and buyer (for example, 1% each), or the owner may cover the full 2%. The agreed fee allocation must be confirmed in writing before the service begins. Any split must be agreed by the parties before the service proceeds and does not guarantee acceptance of an offer. The scope of work and any applicable additional charges will be confirmed in writing. MOU and transaction documentation support will be provided through an appropriately authorised professional partner where required; this is not a substitute for independent legal advice.
